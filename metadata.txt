about=Shows Traccar GPS devices on the map (markers and tracks) for one time window — last N minutes, custom dates, or a feature's start/end — refreshed live in the background, drawn on the map or as temporary layers. Save positions (latest fix or every fix) and tracks (one line per device, Z = altitude, M = time) to your own layers in any CRS. Same layer schema as the QField plugin Traccar_QField. Needs a Traccar server account.
