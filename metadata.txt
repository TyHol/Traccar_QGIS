description=Follow Traccar devices on the map within a chosen time window, and save their positions and tracks to project layers
about=Shows Traccar GPS devices on the map (markers and tracks) for one time window — last N minutes, custom dates, or a feature's start/end — refreshed live in the background, drawn on the map or as temporary layers. Save positions (latest fix or every fix) and tracks (one line per device, Z = altitude, M = time) to your own layers in any CRS. Same layer schema as the QField plugin Traccar_QField. Needs a Traccar server account. Not an official Traccar plugin: independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by Traccar. Traccar is open-source GPS tracking software by Anton Tananaev and contributors (www.traccar.org).

    0.4.2 - Save geofences to polygon / line layers; save automatically after every refresh (long tracks back off). Sent points and geofences stay on the map until Traccar has them. Areas and lines drawn while you add them; adding tools end by themselves (Cancel / Esc).

Devices / Geofences tabs with hideable columns. Not-an-official-Traccar-plugin notice.
    0.3.0 - Dockable panel (docked, tabbed or floating). Location column (lat/long, project or custom CRS, Irish / UK grid); copy a location (also what3words, WKT) or a track (WKT) to the clipboard. Playback of the tracks in the time window (go to a time, pan to and copy the position shown).
